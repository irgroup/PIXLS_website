The PIXLS Project & Scope

Preprint servers have transformed scientific communication in the life sciences, enabling immediate dissemination of groundbreaking results. However, traditional digital library systems struggle to index the rich multimodality of preprints.

The Challenge: Dark Data in Scientific PDFs

Scientific literature in biomedicine is inherently multimodal: the most essential experimental data, dosage tables, statistical results, and molecular observations are embedded within tables, figures, and visual layouts. Standard full-text search engines treat PDFs as flat strings of text, completely losing structural context and rendering tables and figures invisible to retrieval algorithms.

Furthermore, evaluating retrieval systems on new tasks requires costly human relevance judgments. Test collections age quickly, failing to reflect modern user requirements such as complex question-answering and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

The PIXLS Solution: Multimodal AI & Open Science

Funded by the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) in the LIS program (Wissenschaftliche Literaturversorgungs- und Informationssysteme), PIXLS tackles both dimensions:

  • ✓ Automated Ingestion Pipeline: Continuous harvesting of 530,000+ preprints from bioRxiv, medRxiv, and 30+ OSF archives.
  • ✓ Vision-Language Parsing: Moving beyond brittle legacy rule-based tools to Docling and modern Vision-Language Models (dots.mocr) with reanimator-vlm.
  • ✓ Synthetic Relevance Benchmarks: Generating validated multi-modal relevance labels using LLMs (UMBRELA framework) to revive legacy test collections (TREC-COVID+).
  • ✓ Generative Multimodal Retrieval: Experimental evaluation of RAG systems (MIRAGES) showing that tables and context significantly improve answer reliability.

Research Pillars & Work Packages

PIXLS structures its innovations across five coordinated work packages executed jointly by TH Köln and ZB MED.

Data Acquisition AP 1

An asynchronous, fault-tolerant ELT pipeline that continuously harvests preprints and structured metadata from over 30 international repositories using REST APIs with leaky-bucket rate limiting.

  • 530,000+ life science preprints
  • bioRxiv, medRxiv, and 30+ OSF servers
  • Staging area & automated test suite
  • ZB MED infrastructure service
Multimodal Extraction AP 2

Deep multimodal document parsing of scientific PDFs using Docling and modern Vision-Language Models (VLMs), extracting structured text, machine-readable tables, figures, captions, and in-text references.

  • Powered by reanimator-vlm
  • In-text reference contextualization
  • Structured relational storage
  • Independence from closed LLM APIs
Knowledge Integration AP 3 & 4

Homogenization and persistent enrichment of preprint metadata in the ZB MED Knowledge Environment (KE), combined with semantically enriched BERT embeddings for publication classification.

  • ZB MED Knowledge Environment (KE)
  • Enriched embeddings (NSLP 2024)
  • LIVIVO discovery preparation
  • FAIR metadata exposure via REST APIs
Synthetic IR & RAG AP 5

Revitalizing test collections using LLMs/VLMs for synthetic relevance judgments, and evaluating multimodal RAG systems (MIRAGES) with parametric weighting between text passages and data tables.

  • ACM SIGIR 2025 accepted framework
  • TREC-COVID+ multimodal benchmark
  • MIRAGES experimental retrieval platform
  • Empirical proof of table impact on RAG

Software & Open Source Tools

PIXLS embraces Open Science. Our primary software release, REANIMATOR-VLM, is openly distributed on PyPI and GitHub with comprehensive documentation.

Featured Toolkit

reanimator-vlm

A clean rebuild of REANIMATOR using vision-language models (such as dots.mocr) for document collection processing, structured multimodal element parsing (tables, figures, formulas, text), and domain-independent synthetic relevance assessment via the UMBRELA framework.

VLM-Powered Extraction High-precision optical recognition of tables, visual elements, and formulas.
Synthetic Judgments Automated, reproducible multi-grade relevance labeling for RAG evaluation.

Python Quickstart 
from reanimator import (
    DocumentCollection,
    MultimodalParser,
    SyntheticAssessor
)

# 1. Load collection of preprint PDFs
collection = DocumentCollection.from_folder("./preprints")

# 2. Parse multimodal components with VLM
parser = MultimodalParser(model="dots.mocr")
parsed_docs = parser.parse(
    collection,
    extract_tables=True,
    link_contexts=True
)

# 3. Generate synthetic relevance for RAG
assessor = SyntheticAssessor(framework="umbrela")
judgments = assessor.evaluate_relevance(
    query="remdesivir clinical efficacy",
    elements=parsed_docs.get_tables_and_passages()
)

print(f"Revitalized {len(judgments)} labeled pairs.")

ISI 2027 • Hamburg

Information Extraction in Practice

Information extraction (IE) has transitioned from research prototypes into everyday practice across libraries, archives, and research data services. While LLMs and VLMs have dramatically lowered entry barriers, deploying robust pipelines in production raises vital questions about quality assurance, data provenance, computational cost, and long-term service sustainability.

Co-organized by leaders from TH Köln, ZB MED, ZBW, and GESIS, this half-day workshop brings together practitioners and researchers from the DFG LIS programme, Leibniz institutes, and NFDI consortia to seed a sustainable community of practice.

Topics of Interest

Pipelines & Multimodal Parsing
  • Extraction from OCR output, historical PDFs, tables, and figures
  • LLM- and VLM-based extraction in production: prompting, fine-tuning, and hallucination control
  • Entity linking to authority files and knowledge graphs (GND, Wikidata, ORKG)
Evaluation, QA & Provenance
  • Evaluation methodologies, gold standards, and benchmark datasets
  • Human-in-the-loop workflows for subject experts and cataloguers
  • FAIR publication, data provenance, and transparency of extracted metadata
Infrastructure & Sustainability
  • Moving IE components from funded DFG/NFDI projects into permanent library services
  • Operational cost, latency, and hardware constraints in production
  • Legal and ethical aspects under German text and data mining copyright law
