Led by Prof. Dr. Philipp Schaer (TH Köln) and Prof. Dr. Konrad U. Förstner (ZB MED), PIXLS aims to make rapidly published preprint literature methodically accessible, extracting tables, formulas, and figures, and establishing next-generation retrieval services for the German and international research communities.
